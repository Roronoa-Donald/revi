2. Formules et Métriques de Disponibilité SLA (Uptime & Downtime)

Calcul du Temps de Panne Toléré

Pour un mois de $D$ jours et un objectif de disponibilité de $T$ % :

$$\text{Minutes Totales} = D \times 24 \times 60$$

$$\text{Downtime Autorisé (min)} = \text{Minutes Totales} \times \frac{100 - T}{100}$$

$$\text{Heures Pénalisables} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Downtime Réel} - \text{Downtime Autorisé}}{60} \right\rceil$$

4. Boîte à Outils des Daily Standups & Rétrospectives Agiles

Daily Standup Meeting Toolkit

  • Yesterday : "Yesterday, I wrapped up the auth refactoring...", "I pushed the PR for database indexing..."
  • Today : "Today, I will focus on writing unit tests...", "I am pairing with Alex on the Redis cache..."
  • Blockers : "I am currently blocked by missing AWS IAM permissions...", "No blockers on my side, all good."
  • Interruption polie : "Could I just jump in for a second?", "If I could add to that..."

Présentations & Balisage Verbal (Signposting)

  • Starting : "Today, I’d like to talk about...", "The purpose of this talk is to..."
  • Outlining : "I have divided my presentation into three main parts..."
  • Transitions : "Moving on to our architecture...", "Turning now to the performance benchmarks..."
  • Concluding : "To sum up, our solution cut latency by 40%...", "I’d be happy to take questions."
