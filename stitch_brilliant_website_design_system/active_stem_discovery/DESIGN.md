## Brand & Style

This design system embodies guided discovery, intellectual clarity, and interactive momentum. Tailored for analytical thinkers, students, and lifelong learners exploring STEM subjects, the interface treats concepts as physical, manipulable objects rather than abstract text.

The overarching aesthetic blends **modern tactile minimalism** with **pedagogical precision**:
- Clean, high-legibility workspaces that reduce cognitive friction.
- Tactile, crisp card surfaces engineered for interactive problem-solving, intuitive diagrams, and micro-feedback loops.
- Purposeful contrast: an uplifting foundation accented by punchy, high-energy success emeralds and focused insight golds.
- Direct, confident, and encouraging tone that celebrates progress, active deduction, and conceptual breakthroughs.

## Colors

The palette balances clinical precision with inviting warmth:
- **Primary (`#21C35E`)**: Brilliant emerald green represents affirmative interaction, correct deductions, primary CTAs, and active progress.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`)**: Amber gold serves as an illumination tone for streaks, tips, hints, interactive states, and milestone achievements.
- **Tertiary (`#2563EB`)**: Cobalt blue indicates exploratory paths, math/logic variables, links, and system indicators.
- **Neutral (`#18181B`)**: Deep charcoal slate ensures sharp contrast across typography and structural borders while avoiding the harshness of pure black.
- **Canvas (`#F7F5F0`) & Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: The warm off-white canvas recedes gently beneath elevated crisp white problem tiles and tool palettes, mitigating eye fatigue during prolonged analytical sessions.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Space Grotesk** for headlines, labels, and mathematical accents to deliver an engineered, scientific identity. **Hanken Grotesk** provides an exceptionally legible, human-centered body text suitable for reading dense conceptual steps and explanations.

- **Scale & Contrast**: Headlines feature tight tracking and prominent weight to anchor questions and interactive challenge prompts.
- **Mathematical & Symbolic Rhythm**: Numbers, variables, and units align cleanly with proportional figures in Hanken Grotesk, while Space Grotesk adds graphic punch to keys, stats, and button labels.
- **Hierarchical Discipline**: Body text maintains a comfortable 1.5–1.55 line-height ratio to prevent mental fatigue across multi-step proofs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an intentional, centered focus model that mirrors interactive work surfaces:
- **Challenge Layout (Single-Focus)**: Max-width container of `768px` for focused challenge sequences, keeping questions, simulations, and response options tightly grouped within the visual field.
- **Dashboard & Course Grid**: Fluid 12-column grid constrained to `1200px` max width for syllabus tracks, performance metrics, and course exploration cards.
- **Rhythm**: Spacing follows a base 4px/8px modular scale. Spacing within questions (`space-md`) stays compact to preserve unit cohesion, while spacing between problem phases (`space-xl`) clearly demarcates transitions.
- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (`< 640px`) shifts layouts into full-width stacked touch cards with anchored bottom interaction trays. Tablet (`641px - 1024px`) maintains side-by-side split screens for complex visualizations and responsive controls. Desktop (`> 1024px`) centers workbenches comfortably.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy emphasizes tactile feedback and crisp structural demarcation rather than dramatic ambient diffusion:

- **Low-Contrast Outlines & Hard Tactile Borders**: Cards, interactive buttons, and step containers utilize crisp `1px` to `2px` borders (`#E4E4E7` resting, `#18181B` active/selected).
- **Physical Push Shadow**: Key action buttons and choice options feature an offset bottom edge (e.g., `0px 4px 0px #18181B` or darkened shade), which collapses upon click/tap to convey tangible physical feedback.
- **Tonal Layering**: Modals and tooltips utilize a pure `#FFFFFF` fill resting atop the `#F7F5F0` background canvas, with light crisp drop shadows (`0 8px 24px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.08)`).
- **Interactive Planes**: Draggable mathematical items, tokens, and sorting blocks lift subtly on hover (`transform: translateY(-2px)`) with crisp containment rings.

## Shapes

The interface employs a balanced, approachable geometry:
- **Base Components**: Cards, interactive choice tiles, input fields, and dialogs utilize `rounded` corners (`0.5rem` / `8px` to `12px`), pairing approachable friendliness with geometric rigor.
- **Containers & Question Canvas**: Major problem wrapper cards use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to feel like cohesive digital chalkboards or interactive tablets.
- **Chips & Status Badges**: Badges, streaks, hints, and step indicators use pill silhouettes (`9999px`) to distinguish meta-information clearly from actionable content tiles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action/Submit)**: Vibrant emerald `#21C35E` background, `#FFFFFF` bold text, rounded shape, with a 3D bottom border shelf (`#16A34A` or `#18181B`) that creates an immediate tactile press response.
- **Secondary (Continue/Explore)**: Surface white with a solid `2px` neutral border (`#18181B`) and bold dark text.
- **Ghost/Hint**: Text-only or light warm tinted background with amber iconography for revealing tips and scratchpad steps.

### Choice Cards & Quiz Options
- Modular interactive tiles with a clean white fill and a `2px` neutral border (`#E4E4E7`).
- **Hover**: Subtle border darkening to `#18181B` with slight upward translation.
- **Selected**: `#18181B` border with light neutral or subtle emerald wash.
- **Correct State**: Border transition to `#21C35E`, light green interior tint, accompanied by a checkmark badge.
- **Incorrect State**: Border transition to `#EF4444`, subtle shake animation, and contextual explanation drawer.

### Progress & Streak Bars
- Segmented pill bars indicating progression through a topic track.
- Filled segments snap into view with a smooth emerald fill; streak counters use amber flame icons and rounded badges.

### Input Fields & Variable Sliders
- Mathematical inputs feature clear monospace-styled glyph alignments, generous padding (`space-md`), and distinct focus outlines.
- Sliders and interactive coordinate controls feature oversized touch thumbs (`24px`) with sharp borders and live numeric tooltip readouts.

### Formula & Diagram Containers
- Neutral white canvases bordered by subtle slate lines (`#E4E4E7`), isolating graphs, canvas-based physics simulations, and SVG geometric constructions for uninhibited user manipulation.
